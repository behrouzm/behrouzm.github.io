Selected projects

MSc thesis · Deep learning

Transformer models for robust epigenetic age prediction

Epigenetic clocks such as the 353-CpG Horvath clock break down when CpG measurements are missing. I trained a feed-forward network, a TabTransformer and an FT-Transformer in PyTorch, with and without missing-data augmentation, and tested them on data with 5–50% of CpGs randomly masked.

3.7 yMAE on complete data (Horvath clock: 7.1 y)
7.6 yMAE at 50% missing CpGs with augmentation (16.6 y without)

PyTorch · Python · cross-validation · data augmentation
